Privacy Policy

High Court of Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh

App: PHHC (com.punjabhighcourtapp) Effective: 24 October 2026 Version: 1.0

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the official mobile application ("PHHC App") of the High Court of Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh, collects, uses, and protects user information. The app is developed and maintained for providing information and online services related to the High Court. By using this application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

This application does not automatically collect personal information about users unless such information is voluntarily provided through forms, registration, or other online services. The information collected may include:

  • Name, email address, contact number, or other information required for providing services
  • Case-related information such as case numbers, party names, or filing details accessed through the app
  • Device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for ensuring compatibility and improving performance
  • Usage data and analytics to improve app functionality and user experience

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing court-related services and information through the application
  • Sending push notifications regarding case updates, cause lists, and important announcements (if enabled by the user)
  • Improving the performance, functionality, and user experience of the application
  • Responding to user queries and providing support

Data Sharing

Your information is used only for official purposes and may be shared only when required by law or with authorized government agencies. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties.

Data Protection

Appropriate security practices are implemented to safeguard data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Information collected through this application is used strictly for official administrative or judicial purposes and is stored in secure systems maintained by authorized personnel.

Important: The information available on this application is intended for general public use and convenience. It should not be treated as a substitute for certified court records.

Cookies & Local Storage

This application may use cookies or local storage to enhance user experience and improve functionality. These are small data files stored on the user's device to remember preferences and analyze usage. Users may disable cookies through their device settings; however, some features of the application may not function properly.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications (such as Google Firebase). These services may collect anonymous usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies. No personally identifiable information is shared with these services without user consent.

User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access the personal information held about them
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their personal data, subject to legal and operational requirements
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time through device settings

Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

Modification of Policy

The High Court reserves the right to update or modify this privacy policy at any time without prior notice. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of the updated policies. Users are advised to review this policy periodically.
